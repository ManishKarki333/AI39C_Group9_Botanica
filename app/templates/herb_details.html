Withania somnifera

Ashwagandha

Ashwagandha is one of the most vital herbs in Ayurvedic healing practices. It has been used for over 3,000 years to relieve stress, increase energy levels, and improve concentration.

Traditional Medicinal Benefits

  • Acts as a powerful adaptogen to modulate biological stress responses.
  • Enhances memory, cognitive executive function, and overall brain health.
  • Supports sleep quality by lowering cortisol markers naturally.

Usage Warnings & Contraindications

Avoid use during pregnancy or lactation periods. May interact with immunosuppressants, thyroid medications, or sedatives. Always consult a certified Ayurvedic practitioner before beginning routine usage.
